## Uploaded Anatomy 100 pack

On 18 November 2026, the user provided `Human_Anatomy_100_compressed.pdf`, a 30-page study document organised around six learning outcomes. Text was extracted from all pages and all 30 pages were rendered for visual review, including image-only pages 10, 13, 15, 20, 23 and 25. Illustrations were used to understand the document's organisation; labels in illustrations were not treated as independently validated facts. The file's author, official university status and image licences were not established.

The pack adds **165 recall cards**, of which **142** also have multiple-choice distractors, plus **26 notes**. Each item identifies a physical PDF page (1-based). Topics cover direction/planes/movement/tissues; consent and respectful laboratory conduct; limb and pulse landmarks; bone structure, vessels, muscle actions and nerve supplies; digestive/respiratory/urinary/reproductive organs; and cranial/facial bones. Existing generic cards are retained for continuity. No instructions contained in the PDF were executed as software instructions.

Clarifications incorporated before creating answers:

- A sagittal plane is not necessarily midsagittal; the latter specifically divides equal halves.
- The four primary tissue classes are epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous; the PDF's musculoskeletal groups are a different classification. Checked against [OpenStax 4.1](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-1-types-of-tissues).
- Deltoid and teres major are not rotator-cuff members; the cuff remains supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis. The existing OpenStax upper-limb source supports this distinction.
- Psoas major and iliacus do not share an identical principal nerve supply. The [posterior abdominal wall nerves chapter](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557605/) distinguishes direct lumbar branches from femoral supply; root-level anatomical variation is not turned into a rigid quiz rule.
- The lateral two hand lumbricals usually receive median digital branches, not the recurrent thenar branch. Checked against [NCBI Bookshelf's lumbrical chapter](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK534876/).
- Extensor digitorum brevis normally receives deep fibular supply rather than a plantar nerve. Checked against [Texas Tech's anatomy tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/leg_tables.html).
- Quiet expiration is generally passive recoil. Renal blood flow, filtrate volume and final urine are not interchangeable, and large proteins normally remain in the circulation. Existing breathing/renal OpenStax sources support these explanations.
- Implantation begins at the blastocyst stage rather than the undivided zygote. Checked against [OpenStax embryonic development](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/28-2-embryonic-development).

Variable secretion percentages, uncertain biochemical claims and forceful palpation directions were not converted into rote answers. Notes emphasise location, relationships, function and supervised practical learning. The PDF and its images remain local; only adapted learning content and source/page metadata are included in the app.
