# Interface assets

Fonts are bundled locally for privacy and offline study. Newsreader and DM Sans are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; the original licenses are in `fonts/`.

- Newsreader: https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader
- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Phosphor regular icons: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core (MIT, license in `icons/`)

`recall-paper.png` is original decorative artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the selected Quiet Studio design. Prompt: a text-free deep forest green banner with a restrained translucent sage folded-paper ribbon on the right and clear space on the left. It is decorative and carries no anatomical information.

`editorial-paper.png` is the locally bundled ImageGen artwork for the selected Editorial Collection design, 2243 × 701 pixels. Prompt: a text-free forest banner, empty left 65%, with one broad finely textured sage paper fold on the right third, matched to the selected mockup. No UI, labels, anatomy or border. It is decorative only.

`ink-paper.png` is an ImageGen palette edit of that artwork for the selected Ink & Linen theme. Prompt: preserve the fold/composition and empty left area, change forest to navy `#23384D` and sage to blue-grey `#D9E2E8` with linen `#F5F2EA` highlights; no added text/UI/objects. The background is slightly cropped in CSS to remove the generated edge margins. The original asset remains available.
